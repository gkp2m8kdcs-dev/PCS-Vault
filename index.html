Accounts & Holdings

Everything You Need to Manage Your Money

A complete personal-finance toolkit, designed to be fast, beautiful and 100% private.

📊

Interactive Dashboard

Total net worth with a 30-day sparkline, monthly income, expense and savings rate, asset allocation donut, top holdings, budget progress and goals — every panel updates live.

💼

Accounts & Transactions

Track checking, savings, credit, cash and crypto accounts. Tap any account to drill into its complete transaction history with categories, notes and receipts.

📈

Holdings & Charts

Record manual price points and inspect custom candlestick charts, cost basis, market value, ROI and unrealised gain over time.

🎯

Budgets & Goals

Weekly, monthly and yearly envelopes per category with rollover and overage colours. Define target and saved amounts with deadline reminders.

🔁

Recurring Bills

Keep subscriptions and rent on schedule. Mark bills paid in one tap and optionally record the payment as a transaction.

📷

Receipt Capture

Snap photos with the camera or pick from your library. Attach multiple images per transaction and view them fullscreen with pinch-to-zoom.

📉

Rich Analytics

Net-worth history, daily cash-flow bars with income vs expense, expense-category pie and weekly income heatmap — all drawn with Core Graphics.

📤

Backup & Export

Optional JSON backup and CSV exports for transactions and holdings. Your data, your format, on your terms.

🎨

Themes & Haptics

Light, dark and system theme, twelve localised currency symbols, haptic feedback and a balance-privacy toggle.

Privacy-First by Design

No account. No login. No remote analytics. No tracking. No advertising SDKs. All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework.
