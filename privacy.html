Privacy Policy

PCS Vault ("the App") is an offline personal finance manager developed for iPhone. We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share any personal data. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Short version: Your financial data never leaves your device. No accounts, no logins, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Data Storage

All account, transaction, holding, budget, goal, recurring bill and receipt data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework and the device's local file system. The data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export a CSV or JSON backup and share it yourself through the iOS share sheet.

Because data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the App will permanently delete all locally stored data. We recommend exporting a JSON backup periodically.

Permissions

The App requests the following iOS permissions, only when needed:

  • Camera — requested only when you tap the "Take Photo" action to attach a receipt to a transaction. You can decline and still use every other feature.
  • Photo Library — requested only when you tap "Choose from Library" to attach an existing image as a receipt.

Both permissions are gated by Apple's standard permission prompts and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → PCS Vault. The App does not access your camera or photos in the background.

Analytics & Tracking

PCS Vault contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, no IDFA usage, and no remote services of any kind. There is no telemetry sent from the App to any server. The App does not contact the internet for any purpose.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or any applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
